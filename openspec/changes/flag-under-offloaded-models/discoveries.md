- REGRESSION, measured 2026-08-13: removing M1's -ngl 99 pin halved throughput 32.6 -> 14.7 tok/s. The planner reserves 2048 MB and delegates the dense split to --fit, targeting 22512 of 24560 MB; M1 needs ~23260 MB at ctx 139264, so --fit shed layers. The pin was overriding the reserve, which is what made it fast. 'Prefer removing the pin' is therefore conditional on fitting the RESERVED budget, not total VRAM. M1 restored to -ngl 99, re-measured 32.64 tok/s.
